# The Pink Lab Notebook — Guardian Field Guide

**Status:** Draft lore and field-note copy; **not yet a shipped in-game UI**. Designed to inform the next dialogue pass and a later collectible notebook implementation.

## A field guide, not a catalogue of monsters to capture

Guardians are Professor Trung's cheerful lab helpers: experimental companions that protect, comfort, clean, warn, or support the Globs. They are known by their behaviors and specialties. The intern learns about each Guardian by meeting it and observing what it does.

A notebook entry should use an **illustrated portrait**, never the existing emoji as final artwork. The user will see the creature's name, function, and one practical tip at a glance, with an optional margin note from Trung. Each entry can add a **Scott sticky note** at a narratively appropriate moment.

### How to read entries

- **Discover at:** existing unlock experiment from the current Guardian catalog. Where a Guardian requires an additional in-game condition, the unlock condition takes precedence.
- **Field role:** literal gameplay function from existing source; text does not promise new mechanics.
- **Field tip:** simple, accessible advice; keep conditional and non-prescriptive.
- **Trung's margin:** optional personality text, never necessary to use the Guardian.

## What else belongs in the Notebook?

### Glob entries

Track each Glob's growth stage (Baby, Grown, Expert), temperament (Shy, Friendly, Energetic), mood/needs, and relevant scientific observations. **Do not invent a roster of collectible Glob species**; the existing game has personalities and stages, not separate species.

### Intruder entries

- **Virus:** An invader that disrupts an otherwise delicate culture. *Field tip:* Watch alerts, intercept threats, and protect the Globs.
- **Creeping mold:** A spreading sign that the culture needs attention. *Field tip:* Cleanup specialists may help.
- **Poisoning:** A health condition requiring care. *Field tip:* Review support equipment and treat the affected Globs.
- **Prism Queen:** First major anomalous Lens pattern, Experiment 4. Curiosity becomes a mystery.
- **Spore Serpent:** Spiral distortion, Experiment 9. The Lens seems to respond to how the lab is configured.
- **Abyss Moth:** Dangerous resonance, Experiment 15. The team's ability to care under pressure matters.
- **Omega Bloom:** Overgrown final resonance, Experiment 21. The player must restore balance rather than defeat evil.

These anomaly names already exist in the current game; their *interpretation* is new story lore, not a new mechanic.

### Discovery pages

- Drawn illustrations of DNA fragments as readings, not literal biological samples.
- One discovery insight per era, not multiple forced lore panels.
- Sticky notes from Scott can appear near notebook tabs to remind the professor about upcoming plans.
- An optional 'My Laboratory' page records the player's chosen name and promotion after Experiment 21.

## Unlock and interaction rules for later implementation

1. A new entry appears after an actual unlock or encounter, not before. A preview may show an unknown silhouette and unlock level.
2. Essential instructions remain visible even for locked or undiscovered items, so the game never punishes a new player for not reading lore.
3. On a Guardian unlock, show only a tiny 'New Notebook Entry' notice. The player chooses when to read.
4. Never stop the dish simulation to force a notebook lesson. In Adventure, the notebook is available between experiments and optionally through a safe in-game panel. If the game is live under a panel, make its running status explicit.
5. Use the same art language and polished frames as the approved Professor Trung and Scott illustrations; portraits should be readable at phone size.
6. Record unlocks with stable Guardian IDs so new art and writing do not invalidate existing saves.
7. Guardians are already selectable at their code-defined levels and may have additional requirements; the notebook does not change availability.
8. Maintain the existing number of Guardian slots (1 initially, 2 from Experiment 8, 3 from Experiment 15).
