💖 How to Play

1. 💧 Keep your Globs hydrated

Tap the dish to place a droplet. Up to 10 may be active at once. Your Globs swim toward the water and grow stronger.

2. ✨ Catch BioBits

Tap the sparkling coins before they disappear to earn BioBits for upgrades and DNA.

3. 🦠 Defend your culture

Tap invaders to sterilize them, or equip a Guardian that protects or heals your Globs.

4. 🧬 Complete experiments

Buy three DNA fragments to advance. You can replay any unlocked experiment and choose a new Guardian team.

🛡️ Guardian Corner
