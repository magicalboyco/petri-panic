💖 How to Play

1. 💧 Keep your Globs hydrated

Tap the dish to place a droplet. Up to 10 may be active at once. Your Globs swim toward the water and grow stronger.

2. ✨ Catch BioBits

BioBits appear as floating reward bubbles. Tap them before they vanish to buy upgrades and DNA. They are not Globs or enemies.

3. 🦠 Know your friendly Globs from contaminants

Your goal is to grow the adorable pink Globs—not the things attacking them! Here is what to look for:

◡💗 Glob: PROTECTRound, pink and smiling. Feed with droplets; do not attack.
✹🦠 Virus: TAPSpiky purple invader that can turn red as it weakens. Tap repeatedly.
✿🍃 Mold: TAPGreen, lumpy contamination. Tap directly until it disappears.

Tap viruses or mold directly to sterilize them. They may require several taps. Tapping empty dish space instead places water; Guardians can also protect or heal Globs.

4. 🧬 Complete experiments

Collect BioBits and buy three DNA fragments. On boss levels, defeat the boss before getting DNA fragment three. The DNA counter gives free DNA during beta testing.

Choose Adventure → Regular or Hard → Experiment → Keepers. Regular wins unlock levels; Hard wins earn separate badges.
