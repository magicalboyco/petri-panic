# Petri Panic — Toronto visual-novel art ledger
Last audited: 2026-11-16 (against live `main` story scene definitions and asset mappings)

**Production rule:** One independent, quality-checked portrait at a time; no contact sheets. Never mark an image as **Live** unless the source asset exists, the scene ID is mapped in `storyRender()`, and the game's hosting deployment passes. Separate *drawn*, *approved*, and *live*. Character consistency, anatomy, recognizable Toronto geography, continuity of cake/roses/tickets/gift, and one appropriate Easter egg per scene are required.

Avoid inventing endorsements or real event appearances.
